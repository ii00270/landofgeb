Bixxville  ·  built by Land of Geb, LLC  ·  Christiansted, St. Croix

Every job in order.
Every checkpoint verified.

Bixxville is the system Land of Geb runs its work on. It breaks a job into checkpoints, puts them in order, and marks each one verified before anyone is paid.

Keeps critical operations running when demand surges and the network fails.

01  —  What it does

Three things,
in this order.

01 · Break it down

Breaks the job down.

Every job becomes a list of checkpoints, each with an owner and an hour estimate, before a price is named.

02 · Put it in order

Puts it in order.

You see what waits on what, what can run at the same time, and which chain sets the finish date.

03 · Verify

Verifies before payment.

A checkpoint is done or not done, against a definition written before the work started. It is paid only when verified.

02  —  Where we're focused

Where we're focused.

Three areas we are building for first. Our focus, not our limit. We are a new company and have not worked in them yet, so we claim no record there.

01

Rescue and disaster recovery

Putting crews, supplies and calls in order after a storm or a failure.
02

Ports, distribution and logistics

Keeping berths, cargo and trucks moving when volume spikes.
03

Refineries

Contractor crews and workforce planning for turnarounds and outages. Our focus there is the contractor and workforce side.

The services are on landofgeb.net. Start with a Readiness Decomposition: it shows where an operation breaks first.
