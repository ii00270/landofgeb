03  —  Advancement

There is no shortcut,
because none was built.

Standing is recomputed at every cycle close on one formula, applied identically to everyone.

The standing formula
R = 0.50·Delivery + 0.25·Timeliness + 0.15·Streak − 0.10·Disputes
Delivery caps at 1.00. Streak caps at nine cycles. Dispute rate caps at five per six cycles. There are no hidden inputs and no discretionary adjustment.

Standing bands run from provisional at entry up through the network's highest tier of trust. Where a member sits is visible to them at all times, and it moves only on the formula above.

No member advances by recruitment or fee payment. Advancement is earned only through verified, delivered work. This is written into the network's constitution as a member right — not offered as a policy that could quietly change later.
Standing never touches active work. It does not direct, pace, or discipline you during an engagement. It applies only to future matching eligibility, after the engagement concludes — and it's scored against a small collapsed set of checkpoints, never against the fine-grained pricing breakdown.
04  —  How work reaches you

Offered, not assigned.

01
An engagement is decomposed
Before anyone is approached, the work exists as a graph of defined units, each with an hour estimate and a tier.
02
The system matches on record
Units route to members whose computed standing and lane fit the work. Matching is on the record, not on relationships.
03
You see the full terms first
Rate, hours, deliverable definition, and window — all fixed and disclosed before you decide. Declining costs you nothing.
04
You do the work
Coordination happens inside the OS by text and voice, next to the task. No pace enforcement, no automated supervision.
05
It closes binary
Done or not done, against the definition written at scoping. Hours credit only to the verified assignee — the person who actually did it.
06
You're paid in what you elect
Bank transfer, card, cash, or a digital asset if you want one. Nobody is ever compelled to take crypto in place of fiat.
05  —  Lanes

Peers by lane,
not by rank.

Members holding equal standing within a shared lane of work constitute a node. The distinction between peers is the lane they run — not a hierarchy over one another. Work supply and labor supply both count toward standing inside a node.

06  —  Governance

Who decides what.

All governance decisions — including the pace, prioritization, and discipline of labor — are made by human members acting in a governing capacity. The runtime's role is limited to matching, pricing, decomposition assistance, and keeping the historical record. It does not supervise, pace, or discipline anyone in real time.

→Land of Geb, LLC conducts operations, engages members, and executes on behalf of the network.
→Whoever brings the work holds the rights and the responsibility for that engagement. The Executive Operator controls intake and owns the system's fairness — not every engagement's individual risk.
→Governance is human. No transaction inside the network is decided by a single interested party, and no automated process sets the pace or discipline of anyone's labor.
