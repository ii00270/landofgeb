Seven questions, and you get a map.

Answer these and we send back the first steps and the order that matters, before anyone names a price. The map is yours to keep, whether or not we work together.

1
What is the goal?
In your own words: what are you trying to make happen?
2
What does "done" look like?
How you will know it worked.
3
When does it need to be ready?
The date, and why that date.
4
What is already in motion?
What you have started, tried or finished.
5
What is the biggest obstacle?
The hard part, the unknown, the thing that keeps stalling it.
6
Who is involved?
Who does the work, who signs off, who has to say yes.
7
How do we reach you?
Name, where you are, email.
