Introduction

Keeps critical operations running when demand surges and the network fails.

Operations break under overload: too much information, too many handoffs, too little capacity at the moment it matters. We find where it builds up and take it out, in order.

How it starts

  1. A conversation. You tell us the operation and the date you need it ready by.
  2. A Readiness Decomposition. We break the operation into tasks, dependencies and labor hours, and mark where it will overload first.
  3. A report you can read once: the weak points ranked, and the order to fix them.

How we price

We size the work first, then name one flat price. The working behind the number is yours to see before you sign.

Scope. Planning, analysis and administrative services. Not engineering, safety certification, legal, financial or medical advice.
